## [1.0.0] - 2026-11-26

- Initial release of Warp for the Droppy Store.
- Native macOS networkQuality engine measuring download, upload, and latency with zero external dependencies.
- Animated 270-degree speedometer gauge with phase-aware gradient illumination and monospaced rolling digits.
- Bento-style Expanded Surface dashboard with enlarged 180pt gauge and modular telemetry tiles.
- Comprehensive network diagnostics: bufferbloat loaded latency, Apple Responsiveness RPM ratings, and TCP/TLS handshake timings.
- Fluid morphed transitions expanding from the notch HUD and collapsing back seamlessly.
- Shelf widget supporting both Solo (notch wrap with frosted glass GO button) and Paired compact layouts.
- In-view persistence keeping results displayed while open, automatically resetting after 14 seconds out of view.
- Compact completion HUD notification banner with one-click details takeover.
- Configurable speed units (Mbps and MB/s), HUD notifications toggle, and local expandable test history.
- Non-blocking rotating diagnostic logging in `~/Library/Logs/Warp/` with in-app zip export and Finder reveal.
